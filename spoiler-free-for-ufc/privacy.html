Privacy Policy & Terms — Spoiler Free for UFC

Operated by Trantor Tools · Last updated: November 2026

1. Overview

Spoiler Free for UFC is a browser extension developed and operated by Trantor Tools. We respect your privacy and follow a strict data-minimization approach: the extension works locally in your browser and does not track your browsing history, sell data, or display advertisements.

2. Information Stored Locally

The extension uses the standard browser storage API (chrome.storage.sync) solely to save your preferences and license state across your signed-in browser sessions:

  • Extension enabled/disabled toggle state.
  • 30-day free trial start timestamp.
  • Pro license activation status.

3. Website Access

The extension runs exclusively on ufc.com and ufcespanol.com to hide fight results and render reveal buttons directly on the page. No page content or personal browsing activity is transmitted to our servers.

4. Payments & Licensing

Optional Pro subscriptions and payments are securely processed by third-party payment providers (ExtensionPay and Stripe). Trantor Tools does not collect, process, or store your credit card or banking details. Please refer to Stripe's and ExtensionPay's respective privacy policies for details on how payment data is handled.

5. Contact

For support, licensing assistance, or privacy inquiries, please contact us via email (preferred channel): trantor.tools@gmail.com.

6. Terms of Service & Service Availability

Spoiler Free for UFC is provided on an "as is" and "as available" basis. Because the extension relies on the layout and code structure of third-party websites (ufc.com and ufcespanol.com), uninterrupted compatibility cannot be permanently guaranteed if those sites undergo major structural changes.

Trantor Tools reserves the right to modify, suspend, or discontinue the extension or its Pro subscription service at any time. In the event of permanent discontinuation, active recurring subscriptions will be canceled so no further billing cycles occur, and users will retain access through the end of their current paid billing period.
